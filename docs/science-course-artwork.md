# Water and Weather artwork

The course uses a paper-cut and gouache field-guide visual language inside Mehrdadika's existing interface. The atmospheric cover is separate from the editable teaching diagrams, whose labels and geometry communicate the science.

The cover is `public/courses/water-weather/cover.webp`. It was generated with the built-in Image Gen tool and converted from PNG to WebP with `cwebp -q 88`. No existing image was replaced. The source output remains in the generation tool's original storage. The SVG diagrams are original code-authored educational graphics. All course artwork is AI-assisted editorial-preview material pending independent science review.

Final cover prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide illustrated cover for an English-learning science course called Water and Weather. Create a beautiful tactile editorial paper-cut and gouache field-guide illustration, landscape composition 3:2. A small harmonious landscape of deep blue water, a winding stream, pale layered mountains with a little snow, soft white clouds, a golden sun and lush sage-green land; a few stylized raindrops in one area. Rich cut-paper edges and delicate watercolor grain, sophisticated and calm, suitable for teenage and adult learners. Palette indigo, dusty sky blue, warm cream, soft sage and muted ochre, on warm paper. Coherent engaging composition with depth, no human figures. This is an atmospheric course cover, not a scientific process diagram: no arrows, no particle models, no labels, no letters, no typography, no logos, no watermark, no UI. Water vapor is invisible; clouds should look like soft condensed clouds, not colored gas rising from water. Render a finished polished illustration that can sit inside a rounded card.

Each future course can declare its own `visualStyle` and artwork while using the same application components, typography, navigation and interaction rules.

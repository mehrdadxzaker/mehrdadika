## Pictures and review

All 24 new illustrations are original assets generated individually with the built-in image tool and encoded as 512px WebP images. The author and a second AI reviewer inspect actual full-size pixels plus final 512px and 120px versions. [Media provenance](../content/media-provenance.json) records current literal generation/edit prompts, licensing and hashes. The [illustration audit](../content/illustration-audit.json) binds semantic and style findings to final bytes, selected senses/examples and the exact category-profile hash. These are AI inspections, with independent human language and specialist review still pending.

Clinical artwork uses cool white with restrained mint and pale blue. The bacterium selects one rod-shaped cell with a loose nucleoid and no membrane-bound nucleus. Virus selects a generic enveloped particle with a separate protein shell and internal nucleic acid; no specific pathogen or exact genome structure is implied. Fungus selects a nucleated budding yeast, rather than suggesting all fungi share this shape. Antibody uses a qualitative four-chain IgG monomer. An initial candidate placed its binding pockets only on the heavy chains and was rejected; a built-in edit extended the light chains to form combined heavy/light tip interfaces. Antigen instead emphasizes a recognized patch on a dominant protein molecule, with one smaller antibody arm tip bound to it. A language review replaced the ambiguous collocation “an antigen binding region” with “a surface antigen” in all editions.

Vaccine distinguishes a preparation vial from the conceptual antigen-recognition and memory inset. It does not assume a live-virus formulation or guaranteed protection. Allergy selects a possible pollen-related nasal/eye reaction; allergen selects the possible trigger source, with an enlarged pollen example. Neither appearance nor a schematic inset establishes a diagnosis, sensitization, species or clinical result. Parasite selects an external head-louse example on host hair; an initial five-leg candidate was corrected to six legs. Parasites are not all insects. Lymph node, palatine tonsil and red bone marrow select specified immune-system structures. A bone-marrow candidate with invented external pelvic holes was rejected and corrected to a local iliac fragment with a continuous outer cortex. Molecular colors, magnification, organelle proportions and representative anatomy are explanatory, not calibrated measurements, specimens or examination findings.

Engineering artwork uses precise silver technical forms, subtle cutaway shading, slate ground and restrained amber teaching cues. Evaporator and condenser distinguish the direction of phase change and heat transfer; contradictory heating arrows in the first evaporator candidate were corrected. Their essential LIQUID/VAPOR and HEAT IN/HEAT OUT labels were checked character by character in the full and 512px images. Expansion valve selects a refrigerant-metering component. Earlier near-contact and solid-post candidates were rejected; the final correction routes the inlet below a continuous partition and the outlet above it, through a visible fixed ring seat with needle clearance. HIGH/LOW mean qualitative relative pressure, not measured values or a phase code. It is not a device that creates cooling without a supplied system. Heating element selects an unpowered sheathed spiral with ceramic-insulated terminals, rather than showing an exposed resistance wire. The text was aligned with those visible bushings and the hidden internal wire. Fin selects one projecting heat-transfer plate joined to a shared base, with neighboring fins providing context.

The heat pipe has a sealed wall, continuous wick, central vapor path toward the cooled end and opposite liquid-return paths along the wick. Steam trap selects a ball-float mechanism linked to a submerged outlet valve. Earlier candidates did not isolate the outlet gallery from the upper vapor space; targeted built-in edits created a floor-to-roof partition with one controlled submerged orifice. The illustration remains a qualitative mechanism, without certified sealing, exact kinematics, venting arrangements or installation dimensions. Exhaust pipe has physically connected engine, pipe, muffler and tail context. Initial and first-edited candidates retained contradictory internal arrows and were rejected; the final edit removes the interior strip and leaves one outward tail arrow. Combustion chamber selects the bounded volume above a piston, injector a nozzle-and-needle component with a qualitative droplet spray, and exhaust manifold connected branches collecting into one outlet. The heat-pump loop has consistent circulation and opposing heat-input/output relationships with explicit supplied compressor power. Its POWER IN, COOLER and WARMER labels were checked at full size and 512px. No dimensions, fuel quantities, thermal ratings, emissions, wiring or buildable designs are supplied.

Older red blood cell and white blood cell illustrations were actually inspected and retained because their distinguishing morphology and clinical style remain clear. The white-cell image represents its already-qualified neutrophil example, not every leukocyte. This batch adds **24 new pictures and zero legacy replacements**. All older canonical and immutable media remain available. Rejected drafts and their negative inspection evidence remain private.
